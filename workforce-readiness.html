The staff member asks: do I have the roles, skills, tools and systems to do the job — and will the change be supported? The evidence is already collected. Dimension 2 requires a role/competency map with gaps, a skills pipeline and succession records; Dimensions 10 and 11 add change appetite and delivery muscle; and every element carries a Knowledge-Continuity rating flagging single-person dependency. The raw material for a role-gap register exists — this view rolls it up.

The single largest cause of low maturity is not technology. Ownership (X.G1) binds 33 items and knowledge continuity (X.G2) binds 19 — 52 of the 158 Intervene items. Nobody owns the thing; the thing dies with the individual. This is why Wave 0 assigns owners across 193 items and runs a documentation sprint before any system is touched — and why the workforce risk (R2, R9) sits high on the Capability Risk Register.
