One figure to reconcile before the C3 snapshot is frozen. The Phase 2 report published an overall PEAR of 26.3, computed against a legacy proxy. The correct practice definition (0.6 × AMI + 0.4 × ERI) gives 34.23 at C2. Unless the Phase 2 workbook is corrected, the delta engine will report a false improvement of about eight points between C2 and C3. The trajectory above uses the corrected 34.23 baseline.
The most important row is the gate. W0 resolves no architecture items and moves no element score — and it is still the highest-priority wave, because nothing in C3 is deliverable if EE Dimension 11 does not change from NOT CLEARED to CLEARED. The honest consequence: the C3 target still leaves a majority of elements in Intervene. That is the readiness gate working, not failing.
