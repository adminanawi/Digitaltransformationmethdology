SITA and the GITO ask which systems must be provisioned, hosted and integrated, and where spend is duplicated. The System Rationalisation Roadmap dispositions the application estate — what is retired, replaced, consolidated or retained — in what sequence, and why. Its governing rule is absolute: nothing is retired before its replacement exists.
