The GITO/GITOC and SITA ask whether the ICT estate is coherent, secure, interoperable and rationalised. The Technology-Layer Catalogue is the three technology elements — Internal (hosting), Integration (the interoperability layer) and External (the dependencies) — catalogued and scored. It is the weakest layer in the department, and the one the whole roadmap turns on.

Behind these three scores sits the estate itself: departmental and SITA-hosted infrastructure, the absent enterprise service bus, and the external dependencies (Home Affairs NPR, the Deeds Registry, SASSA, SARS) that today are reached by email, batch file or re-keying rather than by governed API. The System Rationalisation Roadmap dispositions every application on top of this layer.

Technology Integration carries maximum weight and near-minimum score. At 0.75 against a weight of 2.0, it is the single largest drag on the weighted index. The enterprise service bus and API gateway built in Wave 1 is what converts absent and manual exchanges into governed, MIOS-conformant APIs — the one fix that lifts eight elements above it.
