National Treasury and the Minister ask: is this ICT spend justified, is it duplicated elsewhere, and what is the risk of not funding it? The Strategic Investment Case answers strategic question 4 (X1007 §8) — the cost and the funding route, tested against PFMA mechanisms. Costs are order-of-magnitude planning figures created in Phase 3: sufficient to sequence and to test affordability, not a substitute for a procurement estimate.

The proportion is the argument. R512m over four years is about R128m a year, against a Vote 33 baseline that transfers R30.9 billion a year in conditional grants presently monitored on a spreadsheet. A Strategic Investment Case is mandatory for FA-3, FA-4 and FA-7, each of which exceeds the ICT procurement threshold. The funding routes are named per PFMA mechanism — MTEF reprioritisation, operational budget or protected line — because "who pays, and how" is part of the answer, not an afterthought.
