A baseline, then five phases

Digital transformation is a combination of Enterprise Architecture and the positioning and availability of enabling capabilities to ensure that what is envisioned by the architecture has a high likely-hood of being successfully implemented.

Phase 0 is where a department starts. It is a 30-question maturity questionnaire, completed in a single structured interview, that establishes how far the department currently is from the 3.0 target. It is the cheapest possible entry point into the method and it defines exactly what the five phases must close.

What every element is scored against

Across the whole method, maturity is judged on a 0–5 ladder and classified into four zones. The rule is worst-dimension-wins: within any one item, the score is the lowest of its ladder rating, its gates and its entity cap — an item is only as mature as its weakest gate. Item scores are then averaged up to the element. The enabling environment uses the same four zones on a 0–4 scale, because its levels describe institutional readiness rather than architectural maturity.

Intervene (0–2) Improve (>2–3) Good (>3–4) Excellent (>4–5)

How to use this set

Work top-to-bottom through the navigation. Start at Phase 0 for the baseline questionnaire. Phases 1 and 2 then build the picture (catalogue and assessment); Phase 3 decides what to do about it; Phase 4 delivers it; Phase 5 governs it. The Enabling Environment page sits alongside them all — it is the readiness check that decides whether the roadmap is even attemptable.
