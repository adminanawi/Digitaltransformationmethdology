The Capability Risk Register records where vendor dependency, skills scarcity, ageing technology, missing integration or institutional weakness create operational risk. Exposure = Impact × Likelihood, each scored 1–5. It is the DG's and the Minister's answer to "what is the risk of proceeding" — and of not proceeding.

The three the board must test. R1 (delivery capability not built, W1 proceeds anyway), R3 (procurement cannot acquire the fabric in time) and R7 (shadow IT retired before its replacement exists) carry exposure at or above 20. They are the risks the Architecture Review Board must test at every gate — because each one, if it materialises, does not slow the programme, it invalidates it.
Why R1 tops the register. It is the hard gate expressed as a risk: commissioning an integration programme into a department with no standing delivery capability is how every previous initiative failed. Mitigation is not a control — it is the sequence itself: Wave 0 builds the capability first.
