This is the only Phase-3 product that speaks in the language of the mandate rather than the estate — the map that goes to the Presidency and Cabinet. For each published outcome it asks a single question: do the capabilities, services and systems exist to deliver on this commitment, and where are the gaps?

Six of the seven published outcomes of the Department of Human Settlements are not assured by the architecture beneath them. The seventh — informal settlements upgrading — is achieved on a capability scoring 1, which is to say it is achieved on personal effort, not on a system.

The worst-served outcome, traced

OUT-03 Security of tenure◄Master Data 11 — title status = 0◄Technology External 15 — Deeds Registry = 0◄No governed interface to the Deeds Office
The chain, read downward. Title deeds are not registered because there is no title-status master data, because there is no governed interface to the Deeds Registry. Fixing the outcome is impossible until the integration fabric (W1) and the golden record (W2) exist. This is why an outcome the Minister answers for cannot be fixed by "trying harder" at the outcome — it is fixed three layers down.
