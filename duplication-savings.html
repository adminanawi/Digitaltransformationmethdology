The dominant saving must be stated honestly. S4 — R46.4m of duplicate and irregular payments prevented — is an avoided loss, not a cash release, and the 0.15%-of-R30.9bn assumption is a Phase-3 estimate that must be replaced with the Auditor-General's actual finding before it is presented to Treasury. Likewise S1–S3 release effort, not cash: in a department scoring 2 on workforce, released effort is redeployed to data stewardship and delivery, not surrendered. It should not be presented as a budget cut.
The real prize is risk removed, not rands. S6 — retiring 73 unbacked-up, unowned, unauditable shadow instances — saves no licence cost and scores R0 in the model, yet it removes a material public-money dependency (Grant_Compliance_2025.xlsx alone monitors R30.9bn).
