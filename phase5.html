Reporting across government

The department's catalogue, scores and dashboards feed a standard cross-government report — so the same 15-element picture can be compared department-to-department, and the centre can see where digital public infrastructure is taking hold and where it is stalled.

The cycle restarts. A rising Architecture Maturity Index (26 → 29) and a growing DPI-utilisation figure become the new baseline. Phase 5's report is Phase 1's starting point for the next assessment cycle — GWEA 2.0 is a loop, not a line.

Hard gate — delivery capability

Architecture risk — what is driving it

The IS Solution and Technology layers carry the highest risk. Data Access (0.5) is the single lowest-scoring element — data is re-keyed at every system boundary. Technology Integration (0.75) and Technology External (0.75) reflect the complete absence of an API gateway and any DPI connection. The Business layer (Service, Process, Application) is at or below 1.55 — manual paper-based processes with no digital channel. The Motivation and Strategy layers score marginally higher (1.64–2.0) but are still firmly in Intervene because outcomes are not operationalised and capability is unmapped.

Enabling environment risk — what is driving it

Two dimensions are in Intervene and block progress. Change & delivery capability (index 25) is the hard gate: the department relies on key individuals with no standing PMO or delivery method — the roadmap cannot advance until this is resolved. Procurement & supply-chain agility (index 25) compounds this: acquisitions are slow, contested and unpredictable, making timely delivery of any initiative uncertain. The remaining eight dimensions sit at Improve (index 50) — present but insufficient — while Executive sponsorship (index 75, Good) is the one stable anchor the transformation can build on.

Reading the risk trajectory. Architecture risk remains at Improve (not Good) even after H3 — because elements such as Outcome, Client and Value Proposition require sustained operational embedding before they reach maturity, not just project delivery.
