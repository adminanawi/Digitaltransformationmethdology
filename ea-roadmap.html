The EA Roadmap converts prioritised findings into a gated sequence. A wave may not start until the previous wave's exit gate is met — progress is measured by deliverables crossing gates, not by time elapsed. It also carries the change-and-adoption plan: an accepted solution nobody uses changes nothing, so adoption is built in, not assumed.

Four gated delivery waves

W0

Clear the gate · Oct 2026 – Mar 2027 · FA-1, FA-2, FA-6

Hard gate uncleared (EE Dim 11 = 1). No architecture change may be scheduled. Stand up the delivery capability, assign owners, and open the procurement route.

Exit gate: PMO and method live; ownership assigned; gate cleared (Dim 11 ≥ 2). Resolves no element score — it resolves the reason nothing else could be resolved.

W1

Fix the root · 2027/28 · C3 · FA-3, FA-8

Gate cleared; procurement route exists; the capability is funded as a whole. Build the MIOS-conformant enterprise service bus and API gateway.

Exit gate: Integration fabric in production. Data Access and Technology Integration begin to move — the two maximum-weight elements.

W2

Build on it · 2028/29 · C4 · FA-4, FA-5

Integration fabric in production; a golden record is not buildable before it exists. Establish beneficiary master data and retire shadow IT.

Exit gate: Golden record live under stewardship; 73 shadow instances retired or consolidated; 103 of 158 Intervene items resolved.

W3

Reach the citizen · 2029/30 · C5 · FA-7

Systems of record exist and are integrated. Only now can a service be digitalised onto something real.

Exit gate: Digital self-service for the eight priority services; processes mapped under the OMF. All 158 Intervene items resolved; entity leaves the Intervene zone.

The sequence is the strategy. W0 resolves no architecture items and moves no element score — and it is still the highest-priority wave, because nothing after it is deliverable without it. The readiness gate is expressed here as a schedule: build the delivery capability and open the procurement route first (W0), fix the shared root cause (W1), build on it (W2), and only then reach the citizen (W3). Closure comes at re-assessment, when the affected element's zone is re-computed — the loop closes only when the citizen feels the difference. Progress is tracked on the Project Gate Tracking board.
