The Operations Management Framework is the single richest source the questionnaire draws on — it compels the Service Delivery Model, business process maps, standard operating procedures, service standards and the service charter. Because those are the very artefacts PEAR catalogues, the OMF Compliance Dashboard falls straight out of the assessment. It answers the Batho Pele / SDIP unit and the DPSA operations governance.

Why process sits at the bottom. Process (1.45) is the department's second-weakest element after the technology layers. The OMF asks for documented, followed, measured processes; the assessment finds them tacit — living in individuals' heads (the knowledge-continuity binding). Process mapping under the OMF is deliberately deferred to Wave 3, once the systems that execute the processes exist — remediating the business layer before the root cause would not hold.
