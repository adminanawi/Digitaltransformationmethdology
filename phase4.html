Note the sequencing discipline: the PMO (hard gate) is deliberately the most advanced project, because nothing ambitious may proceed until it lands. Red here means what red should mean — behind plan. The MIOS integration layer and Transaction Data governance are both genuinely late against their own design milestones, and the dashboard says so rather than recolouring them because a dependency was always going to slow them. The amber on the PMO is the finding that matters most: the one project the entire roadmap waits on is not yet comfortably on track.

What "good" looks like here. A healthy Phase-4 dashboard is honest — it shows red where reality is red — and every project traces to a focus area from Phase 3, which traces to a binding dimension from Phase 2, which traces to a catalogue item from Phase 1. Full line of sight, end to end.
