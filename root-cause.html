The Auditor-General and the DG ask the same question from different heights: are findings resolved at root cause, with an evidence trail? The v15 rule that every rating name an evidence artefact produces the per-element audit trail as a by-product. Root-cause analysis then traces each finding to its architectural cause and collapses 158 Intervene items into eight addressable focus areas.

Two root causes account for most of it. First, ownership and knowledge continuity (X.G1 + X.G2) bind 52 of 158 — nobody owns the thing; the thing dies with the individual. Second, Data Access (0.5) and Technology Integration (0.75) are the shared architectural root: fix the integration fabric once and eight elements below it lift together. This is why the investment case concentrates spend on FA-3, and why remediation is sequenced, not spread evenly.
