The mandate is not an abstraction — it is a stack of Acts and regulations. The assessment is compliance-anchored by design: every element is scored against its specific legislated standard, so the conformance dashboard is a by-product of the assessment, not a separate exercise. It answers the GCIO, the mandate itself, and — through the two lenses below — the MIOS governance and the Information Regulator.

MIOS conformance lens (PSR Reg 97)

Interoperability-standard conformance is compulsory under PSR Reg 97. The interfaces are scored against MIOS through Data Access (0.5) and Technology Integration (0.75) — both at maximum criticality weight, both in Intervene. 30 of 39 catalogued exchanges move by hand, paper, flash drive or email. Conformance is achieved only once the integration fabric (W1) is built.

Data-protection lens (POPIA · PAIA)

POPIA is scored at element level within the compliance-anchored assessment; Data Access findings expose unlawful data movement via scheduled dumps and removable media. PAIA s14 requires a published manual of services and records. Both are carried in the assessment already — this lens maps the POPIA findings and data-flow risks to the PAIA obligation for the Information Regulator.

Why every standard reads Intervene at C2. Worst-dimension-wins again: an element conforms to a standard only when its weakest relevant gate does. The estate will clear these standards element by element as the four waves land — MIOS and POPIA first, because Data Access is the shared root cause.
